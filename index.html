About Milky Bites 💛

A student-managed snack business created to bring delicious and affordable treats to our community.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide delicious, soft, and affordable milk-powdered donut bites made with quality ingredients. We aim to give students and customers satisfying snacks that are easy to enjoy and gentle on the budget.

🌟

Our Vision

To become a well-loved snack brand recognized for its soft texture, rich milky taste, and friendly service, while introducing exciting new flavors and reaching more customers.

🚀

Our Goals

Build loyal customers, maintain quality and cleanliness, keep prices affordable, introduce new flavors, and develop teamwork and entrepreneurial skills.

📖 Our Company History

Milky Bites started as a small student business by BSIT-2C. Our team wanted to create a simple, tasty, and budget-friendly snack for students, teachers, and the local community. Through teamwork and product testing, we developed our bite-sized treat and worked to improve its taste, texture, packaging, and presentation. We aim to grow through quality products, customer feedback, and friendly service.

Ingredients & Preparation 🥛

Made with care for a soft and delicious milky snack.

🧺

Main Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Milk powder
  • White sugar
  • Cooking oil
  • Chocolate/Cheese
  • Evaporated milk
👩‍🍳

Preparation Process

  1. Mix the ingredients to form a soft dough.
  2. Shape the dough into small, uniform balls.
  3. Deep-fry in clean, medium-hot oil until golden.
  4. Coat the warm bites with milk powder.
  5. Place them in clean packaging, ready to serve.

Customers with food allergies should confirm the complete recipe and possible cross-contact with the seller.
